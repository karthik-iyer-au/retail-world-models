\section{Data, model versions and protocol}
\label{sec:versions}

\paragraph{Data}
The four public data sets of the companion paper~\cite{rwm}. \emph{M5} holds
daily Walmart sales of 3,049 items in 10 stores from 2011 to 2016, with prices,
event days and SNAP days~\cite{m5accuracy}. \emph{Dominick's Finer Foods} holds
weekly scanner data by store for a Chicago chain from 1989 to 1997, with price,
a promotion code and cost~\cite{dominicks}; the companion paper's six
categories are audited one by one (cheese, snack crackers, soft drinks, bottled
juice, analgesics, frozen entrees). \emph{Breakfast at the Frat} holds weekly
sales of 55 breakfast items in 77 stores from 2009 to 2012, with display,
feature and tag-only price reduction flags~\cite{frat}. \emph{Corporaci\'on
Favorita} holds daily sales of an Ecuadorian grocery chain to August
2017~\cite{favorita}: the 10 stores with the most units in 2016 crossed with the
items sold in all ten, with the promotion flag, holidays, events and paydays,
and no prices. That makes nine data sets. No raw data is redistributed.

\paragraph{Models audited}
The audit reads the world model the companion paper reports for each data set:
settings tuned by a search on that data set, fitted on its rolling windows (six
on each Dominick's category, four on Frat, M5 and Favorita), each the average of
five copies fitted with different seeds, with the fitted model of every window
saved. LightGBM with its own tuned settings, refitted on the same windows, gives
the comparison for C6. The question of whether passing the checks goes with
accuracy uses the versions the settings searches saved: every change tried on
every data set, one seed when screened and three when confirmed, each with a
fitted model per search window and its search-window accuracy. The audit of the
companion paper's first-version models, made in October 2026, stays in the
repository's record; this paper reports the tuned models.

\paragraph{Protocol}
The audit reads models and never tunes them. Every check runs on every version
it applies to and every result is reported, including failures. The plan, its
thresholds and every amendment are dated in
\texttt{docs/PAPER2\_PLAN.md}; each amendment was made before the results it
affects were read.
