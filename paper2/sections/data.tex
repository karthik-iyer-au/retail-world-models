\section{Data, model versions and protocol}
\label{sec:versions}

\paragraph{Data}
The three public data sets of the companion paper~\cite{rwm}: M5, daily
Walmart sales of 3,049 items in 10 stores from 2011 to 2016, with prices, event
days and SNAP days~\cite{m5accuracy}; Dominick's Finer Foods, weekly scanner
data by store for a Chicago chain from 1989 to 1997, with price, a promotion
code and cost~\cite{dominicks}, of which the companion paper's six final
categories are audited (cheese, snack crackers, soft drinks, bottled juice,
analgesics, frozen entrees); and Breakfast at the Frat, weekly sales of 55
breakfast items in 77 stores from 2009 to 2012, with display, feature and
tag-only price reduction flags~\cite{frat}. No raw data is redistributed.

\paragraph{Model versions}
Every saved world-model and lift-model checkpoint from the companion paper is
audited: the development and final windows of Frat, the development versions on
Dominick's, and the M5 practice, confirmation and test windows. The Dominick's
final world models, each an average of three fits, were not saved in the
companion paper; they are refitted with settings unchanged for the audit,
three seeds each, and the companion paper keeps the scores of its original
runs. Each version's accuracy is read from its own run record. LightGBM is
refitted on the Dominick's and Frat final windows and the M5 test month for
C6, with the settings of the companion paper.

\paragraph{Protocol}
The audit reads models and never tunes them. Every check runs on every version
it applies to and every result is reported, including failures. The plan, its
thresholds and every amendment are dated in
\texttt{docs/PAPER2\_PLAN.md}; each amendment was made before the results it
affects were read.
