\section{Reproducibility, compute and licence}
\label{sec:repro}

Every number, table and figure in this paper is generated from committed
records by \texttt{make -C paper2}, and a continuous integration job rebuilds
the paper from a fresh checkout on every change and fails on any LaTeX error,
undefined reference or missing font. The checks are in
\texttt{src/rwm/audit/}; the zone detector and estimators in
\texttt{src/rwm/audit/zones.py}; the generated data in
\texttt{src/rwm/data/synthetic\_levers.py}. The runs:
\begin{description}\setlength{\itemsep}{1pt}
\item[\texttt{scripts/zone\_episodes.py}] lists the episodes and their
responses (\nolinkurl{results/paper2/zone_episodes.csv});
\item[\texttt{scripts/zone\_robustness.py}] the event study, placebo and
sensitivity;
\item[\texttt{scripts/known\_answer.py}] the known-answer study;
\item[\texttt{scripts/audit\_runs.py}] and \nolinkurl{scripts/paper2_colab.sh}:
the audit of the saved versions, C6 and C7;
\item[\texttt{scripts/audit\_diagnose.py}] the diagnosis of C1 and C4
(\nolinkurl{results/paper2/diagnose/});
\item[\texttt{scripts/audit\_summary.py}] the tables committed under
\texttt{results/paper2/}.
\end{description}
