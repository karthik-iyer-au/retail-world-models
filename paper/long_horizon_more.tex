\paragraph{Added after the planned test: other designs and other models}
After seeing the planned result we added two designs in which no model takes in
its own forecasts, and ran the other models of the paper over 52 weeks
(Table~\ref{tab:long-horizon-all}). In the first, the world model rolls its
state forward 13 weeks at a time on the plan; in the second, LightGBM fits one
model per block of 13 weeks. Rolling on helps on M5 and fails on Dominick's. On M5
the world model rolled on scores \lhMfiveWmRollon{} against \lhMfiveWmBlocks{} in
blocks, and its average with LightGBM, \lhMfiveAvgRollon, is the most accurate
forecast on M5 and clearly more accurate than LightGBM forecasting directly
(\lhMfiveLgbDirect) in \lhMfiveAvgRollonLgbBetter{} of
\lhMfiveAvgRollonLgbN{} paired comparisons, clearly less in none. On the
Dominick's categories the rolled-on state drifts: on cheese the forecast is
\lhRollonCheeseFa{} times what sold and the WRMSSE \lhRollonCheese. LightGBM by
block is close to LightGBM forecasting directly. Of all the forecasts in Table~\ref{tab:long-horizon-all}, the average of the
two models forecasting directly is the most accurate on \lhAllLowestAvgDirect{}
data sets, and the average with the world model rolled on on M5. On analgesics
and soft drinks the most accurate is LightGBM fitted per block with settings
searched again for 52 weeks, and on cheese the average of that LightGBM with the
world model; both were added after the planned test. TFT, TimesFM, Chronos and the seasonal naive forecast are
behind LightGBM and the average on every data set; TimesFM given the plan is
ahead of the world model alone on analgesics. Timestral given the plan cannot
forecast 52 weeks: its forecast is \lhTimestralFaOneLo{} to \lhTimestralFaOneHi{}
times what sold in the first 13 weeks and \lhTimestralFaLo{} to
\lhTimestralFaHi{} times over the year. On M5 the world model is scored rolled on and in
blocks of 13 weeks.

\paragraph{Settings searched again for 52 weeks (added after the planned test)}
Each model's settings were searched again for 52 weeks on the six Dominick's
categories, with the paper's search (Section~\ref{sec:loop}) on three 52-week
windows 13 weeks apart. The world model's searches kept one change, on snacks;
LightGBM's kept changes on five categories,
none on cheese. Retuned, the world model is clearly less accurate than LightGBM
in \lhPTunedWmLgbWorse{} of \lhPTunedWmLgbN{} paired comparisons and clearly more
in \lhPTunedWmLgbBetter; the average of the two is clearly more accurate than
LightGBM in \lhPTunedAvgLgbBetter{} and clearly less in \lhPTunedAvgLgbWorse.
Retuning did not help LightGBM on balance: its retuned forecast is clearly more
accurate than the first in \lhPTunedLgbBetter{} comparisons and clearly less in
\lhPTunedLgbWorse.
