To the Editor, \emph{International Journal of Production Economics}

We submit ``Forecasting Retail Sales at Every Grain: Trees, Foundation Models, Transformers and World Models
Compared on Four Public Data Sets'' for consideration as a research article.

Retailers run store orders, promotion plans, category reviews and supplier terms from the same demand at
different grains. The paper asks whether one item-by-store forecast, summed to every coarser grain, can serve
all of these decisions, and which model does it best. It compares gradient-boosted trees, pretrained
foundation models, the Temporal Fusion Transformer and a world model that carries each item's state under the
price and promotion plan, on four public data sets (M5, six Dominick's categories, Breakfast at the Frat and
Corporaci\'on Favorita), with the two main models tuned by the same search procedure.

The findings are stated by decision. The average of the tuned world model and the tuned trees is the safe
default at every grain: lowest error in 22 of 27 grain and data-set cases, never the least accurate on the
periods every final run held out, and the least dollar error in category plans on seven of nine data sets. The
world model alone is the choice for existing items not yet in training, where it loses at most 0.040 in scaled
error against up to 0.311 for the trees. The trees are stronger on heavily promoted and fast-selling lines.
Slow lines, longer training history and year-ahead forecasts give mixed evidence, and the paper says so. The
decision costs of store orders and category plans are reported alongside accuracy, which we believe suits the
journal's readers in operations and supply chain planning.

The manuscript is prepared for double-anonymised review; author details are on the separate title page. An
earlier version is available as a preprint on arXiv (2610.25170). The manuscript is not under consideration
elsewhere. All data are public; the code, configurations and result files are archived on Zenodo
(doi:10.5281/zenodo.24271672). The use of generative AI tools is declared in the manuscript. The authors have no
competing interests and received no funding for this work.

Yours sincerely,
